What is it?

Range Anti-Anxiety is a calculator website for electric vehicle related issues. Users can estimate their car's current range, how much they would need to charge to reach a given distance, how long it would take and how much it would cost. The calculator also has an option for comparing two charging setups. The calculations are only simple estimates due to the very complex nature of all the real world variables involved. The project was completed as the bachelor's project of my degree and it was a group project.
